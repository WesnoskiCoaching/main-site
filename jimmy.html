The R3 System

Three phases. One order.

Most protocols do not fail on ingredients. They fail on sequence. Kill an overgrowth while the nervous system is in fight or flight and the body cannot clear what you killed. Add probiotics before the environment is clean and you are planting seeds in a weed patch.

R1
Phase one

Regulate

Make the body safe to heal. Nervous system and HRV, inflammation down, stomach acid and bile flow restored, sleep and blood sugar steadied, mitochondrial support started, and testing run so the next phase is targeted instead of guessed.

R2
Phase two

Repair

Remove what is driving it. Targeted clearing of what testing actually found, histamine and mast cell reactivity stabilised, the gut lining rebuilt, and the inflammatory load that keeps autoimmune and thyroid patterns active brought down.

R3
Phase three

Rebuild

Build the body you actually wanted. Reseed the microbiome, put mitochondria in the lead, restore hormone and thyroid signalling, and train and eat for recomp, performance or prep. A healthy body is a responsive body.
