Medical Disclaimer

Last updated August 8, 2026

Wesnoski Coaching provides health and performance coaching for educational and coaching purposes only. The information and services we provide are not a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and they do not replace care from a licensed physician.

We are not a medical practice

Wesnoski Coaching is a health coaching practice, not a medical provider. We do not diagnose, prescribe, or treat disease, and we do not provide emergency care. Our coaches read bloodwork against functional reference ranges and build educational protocols covering training, nutrition, supplementation, and lifestyle. Any finding we share is a question to bring to your physician, never a medical directive.

Always consult your physician

Always seek the advice of your physician or other qualified health provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice, or delay seeking it, because of something you have learned through our services. Never start, stop, or change any prescription medication without your physician.

If you think you may have a medical emergency, call your physician or emergency services immediately.

No guaranteed outcomes

Testimonials, case studies, screenshots, videos, and results shared on this website and across our channels are individual experiences and are not typical or guaranteed. Results vary based on your individual circumstances, your starting point, the consistency of your effort, and many other factors. Any changes to prescription medication described in client stories were made by those clients' own physicians. We make no guarantee of any specific result.

Lab testing

Where we facilitate access to private lab testing across North America and the United Kingdom, that testing is provided by third-party laboratories and, where applicable, licensed practitioners. Interpretation we provide is educational and is read alongside, not in place of, your physician's input.

Assessment tools

Any calculators or self-assessments on this site, including the TG:HDL calculator and the MARCH assessment, are educational screens only. They reflect patterns, not clinical findings, and their output is not a diagnosis. Bring any result to your physician and to your coaching assessment, where it is read against your full picture.

Any reliance on the information provided through our services is solely at your own risk.
