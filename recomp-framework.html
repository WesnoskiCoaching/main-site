The Recomp Framework

Why fixing the gut did what years of harder training could not.

Three training days a week on a restricted diet produced the best recomposition of his life. Four reasons why, and how each one applies to a plateau that will not move.

01 of 06

The recomp paradox

Three training days a week at moderate intensity, on a restricted anti-inflammatory diet, no surplus. By every bodybuilding rule that should have gone backwards.

It produced the best body recomposition of his life. Leaner, more muscular, better composition than years of aggressive training had managed.

What he didWhat should have happenedWhat happened
3 days a week, moderateSpinning wheels, losing muscleGot leaner, muscle came back fast
Restricted diet, no surplusNo meaningful composition changeBest recomp of his life

This guide is for the person doing everything right in the gym and getting nothing back. Clean diet, structured programme, disciplined lifestyle, and still bloated, exhausted and stuck on a plateau nothing breaks. The problem is almost never the training. Four reasons why.

02 of 06

Reason 01 · Inflammation was the ceiling

A body cannot build while it is surviving

Chronic gut inflammation locks the body in survival mode. It is not choosing to hold your physique back. It is diverting every available resource to an internal crisis.

  1. Resources go to the crisis, not to building muscle tissue.
  2. Cortisol stays elevated, driving muscle breakdown and fat storage at the same time.
  3. Anabolic hormones get suppressed. Testosterone, IGF-1 and growth hormone all take the hit.
  4. Nutrient partitioning goes sideways. Calories feed survival pathways rather than adaptation.
The insight

When inflammation comes down the ceiling lifts. The same training stimulus that produced nothing starts producing, because the internal environment can finally respond to it.

Signs inflammation is your ceiling

A plateau that ignores calorie adjustments and programme changes. Composition unchanged on consistent training and clean food. Feeling puffy and inflamed while eating well. Recovery that stays slow regardless of sleep. Fullness and hardness never quite arriving.

03 of 06

Reason 02 · Absorption came back online

Eating perfectly means nothing if nothing absorbs

You can eat the most dialled in diet in the world, and if the gut wall is compromised, very little of it reaches the muscle.

Where it breaksWhat is happening
StomachH. pylori suppresses stomach acid. Without acid, protein does not break down properly no matter how much goes in.
Small intestineSIBO ferments food instead of letting it absorb, so calories feed bacteria. Damaged villi cut the absorptive surface area down.
ColonButyrate producers missing means colon cells starve, the lining breaks down and inflammation climbs.

Leaky gut also pulls immune resources away from normal recovery and muscle repair, and candida drives systemic inflammation that impairs intestinal function further.

The insight

Clear the pathogens and repair the lining and absorption restores. The same protein intake that did nothing starts building tissue, without changing a single macro.

04 of 06

Reason 03 · Cortisol normalised

The most underrated body composition driver there is

Most people try to manage cortisol from the outside. If the gut is inflamed, cortisol stays elevated regardless of what you do out there.

Gut inflammation signals danger to the brain along the vagus nerve. The brain raises cortisol against the perceived threat. Cortisol breaks muscle down for emergency energy and parks fat around the midsection. It suppresses thyroid function, which slows metabolic rate. It wrecks sleep architecture, which suppresses anabolic hormones further, and poor sleep drives more cortisol. The loop feeds itself.

StateMuscleBody fat
Chronically elevatedBreakdown accelerated, protein synthesis suppressedVisceral accumulation, lipolysis impaired
NormalisedProtein synthesis upregulated, recovery improvesFat oxidation restores, partitioning improves
The insight

Break the gut inflammation and the cortisol loop breaks with it. Composition shifts without changing a single training variable, because the hormonal environment changed.

05 of 06

Reason 04 · The nervous system shifted

Sympathetic dominance quietly wrecks composition

This is not about mindset. It is about which hormonal environment the body runs in for twenty four hours a day.

  1. Digestion is suppressed in a sympathetic state, with blood flow routed away from digestive organs.
  2. Absorption drops even after pathogens are cleared, if the nervous system stays dysregulated.
  3. Muscle protein synthesis falls under chronic sympathetic activation.
  4. Fat oxidation is impaired while cortisol stays up, and insulin sensitivity drops with it.
  5. Recovery between sessions slows measurably.
The five tools he used
Sunlight

First thing every morning. Sets circadian rhythm, the cortisol awakening response and vitamin D production, which sets the hormonal tone for the whole day before anything else happens.

Cold exposure

Daily cold showers. Builds vagal tone, lowers systemic inflammation, trains the nervous system to change state quickly. Consistency beats intensity here.

Breathwork

Improves CO2 tolerance, oxygenates tissue and shifts the autonomic system out of sympathetic dominance. Before food or training gets the most out of it.

Humming

Vibrates the vagus nerve directly, which is the main line between gut and brain. Free, available anywhere, and five minutes registers.

Meditation

Anchors parasympathetic dominance. Ten minutes daily moves HRV and cortisol regulation over time, and the gut responds faster than most people expect.

06 of 06

Applying this to your situation

If you are stuck on a plateau, ask these before you change the programme.

  1. Bloating or digestive discomfort after meals, regularly.
  2. Energy inconsistent regardless of how much you sleep.
  3. Recovery beyond 48 to 72 hours from a normal session.
  4. Never had a GI Map or an MRT run.
  5. Stubborn midsection fat while otherwise lean.
  6. Composition unchanged no matter how calories or volume move.
The insight

Yes to any of these and the ceiling is internal. The answer is not more training or tighter macros.
