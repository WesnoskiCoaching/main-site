Everything in one file

One place, so nothing gets lost between sessions.

Coaching usually leaks. The plan sits in a PDF, the labs sit in an email, and the question you had on Tuesday is gone by Friday. This closes that gap.

Tr

Training

Your program with sets, loads and video demos. Log the session as you lift and the next block builds from what you actually did.

Nu

Nutrition

Targets and meal structure that move with your phase, not a fixed template. Swap foods without breaking the numbers.

Rx

Supplements

What to take, how much, and when. Timing carries most of the effect on absorption, so it ships as part of the prescription.

La

Labs

Panels scored against functional ranges. Every marker tracked across draws so you see direction, not a single snapshot.

Ck

Check-ins

Weekly data in, a graded review back. Sleep, energy, digestion, training load and weight trend, read together rather than one at a time.

Pr

Progress

Compliance, strength, body composition and the markers that moved. The record your coach reads before every adjustment.

Behind your screen

Your phone is one view of a much larger record.

Every log you enter lands on your case file. Compliance, trend and lab history sit in front of your coach before a single change gets made, which is why adjustments arrive with a reason attached.
