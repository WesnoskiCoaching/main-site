The R3 System

Why the order you heal in matters more than what you take.

Regulate, Repair, Rebuild. The three phase sequence Coach Jimmy Baydoun built from his own autoimmune case, and how to find the phase you actually belong in.

01 of 06

Most protocols fail on order, not ingredients

You have probably tried something already. A probiotic. A gut healing powder. A cleanse. It helped for a few weeks, or it made you feel worse, or nothing happened at all.

The body heals in a sequence. Run the steps out of order and each one fights the one before it.

Kill an overgrowth while the nervous system is stuck in fight or flight and the body cannot clear what you just killed. Add probiotics before the environment is cleaned up and you are planting seeds in a weed patch. Push hard training and an aggressive deficit on an inflamed body and the hole gets deeper.

Jimmy learned this as a competitive bodybuilder who developed an autoimmune condition doing everything by the book. What moved it was not another stack of pills. It was testing, finding the driver in the gut, and fixing things in an order the body could actually follow. That sequence is R3.

The R3 principle

Regulate before you Repair. Repair before you Rebuild. The order of operations is the protocol.

02 of 06

Phase 01 · Regulate

Get the body out of survival mode so it can heal

A body stuck in fight or flight does not prioritize digestion, repair, or fat loss. Stress hormones pull resources away from the gut, blunt stomach acid, slow bile flow, and hold inflammation high. Until that changes, every other intervention is running uphill.

What we work onWhy it comes first
Nervous system and HRVBreathwork, vagal tone, and a daily rhythm the body can predict. Rest and digest is a state, and digestion only happens inside it.
InflammationAn anti-inflammatory food foundation and removal of the obvious triggers, so the immune system stops running an emergency.
Digestive prepStomach acid, stomach lining, and bile flow. Food has to break down and waste has to move before anything else is worth doing.
Sleep and blood sugarSteadier energy through the day and deeper recovery overnight.
Mitochondrial supportStarts here. Cells need raw material to make energy again before they can fund repair.
TestingGI Map and bloodwork where it is warranted, so Phase 2 is targeted instead of guessed.
What people notice

Less bloating and puffiness. Fewer middle of the night wake ups. Less wired, less on edge.

03 of 06

Phase 02 · Repair

Remove what is driving the problem and rebuild the barrier

This is where most people try to start, and it is why so many feel worse or relapse. Clearing overgrowths without a regulated nervous system and open drainage pathways creates more inflammation, not less. Phase 1 did the prep so Phase 2 can do its job.

What we work onWhy now
Targeted clearingOvergrowths and imbalances found on testing, such as dysbiosis, SIBO, candida or H. pylori. Targeted because testing came first.
Mast cell and histamineStabilizing reactivity so food and environmental sensitivities settle instead of spreading.
Gut lining repairMoving from prep into active rebuilding of the barrier between the gut and the bloodstream.
Immune calmingLowering the inflammatory load that keeps autoimmune and thyroid patterns active.
Continued supportNervous system, digestion and mitochondrial work stay in place so the process is tolerable.
What people notice

Clearer skin and fewer food reactions. Consistent, well formed digestion. Less brain fog and steadier mood.

Phase 03 · Rebuild

Reseed, restore, and build the body you actually wanted

With the environment clean and the lining repaired, the body can finally respond. A healthy body is a responsive body. Training, food and recovery start producing results instead of more stress.

What we work onWhat it gives back
Reseed the microbiomeA diverse, resilient gut population that holds, because there is somewhere for it to live now.
Mitochondria take the leadEnergy production, work capacity and recovery become the main focus rather than a floor.
Hormones and thyroidSupporting the systems that were suppressed while inflammation and stress were running the show.
Training and compositionProgressive training and nutrition built for recomp, performance or prep.
Food freedomCarefully widening variety and building habits that hold without a coach standing over them.
What carries forward

Everything. The habits built in Regulate and Repair are what stop the results sliding backward.

06 of 06

Is this you

R3 may be a fit if you are dealing with any of the following and have already been doing the obvious things right.

  1. Gut that will not settle. Bloating, heartburn, irregular digestion, or reacting to foods that used to be fine.
  2. Wired but tired. Poor sleep, a racing mind, waking at 3am with nothing to show for it.
  3. Autoimmune, thyroid or hormone patterns you are managing alongside your doctor.
  4. A physique that will not move. Stubborn body fat, puffiness, and training that used to work and now does nothing.
  5. Skin, brain fog, low energy that nobody has explained to your satisfaction.
  6. Protocols that worked for a while and then stopped.

The first job is finding which phase you actually belong in. Starting in the wrong one is the reason most of this has not worked yet.
