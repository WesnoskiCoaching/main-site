Head Coach · Founder

Farren Brown

15 years in the work · 500+ clients coached · MARCH Method architect

Farren built the MARCH Method after his wife's Hashimoto's kept coming back normal from every doctor she saw. That gap, between what her labs read and what her body was doing, became the practice.

He reads his clients bloodwork against functional ranges rather than standard ones, builds the protocol from what the panel actually says, and sets the standard every coach on this team works to. He does not diagnose and does not prescribe. He works alongside the medical team, not around it.

His caseload runs heaviest on thyroid and Hashimoto's, female hormones and post birth control recovery, gut work, and the cases that have already been through three other practitioners.

The MARCH Method

Five pillars. One order.

Sequence is the protocol. Build the energy floor before you clear anything, clear before you reseed, and restore signalling before you ask the body to perform.

M
Pillar one

Mitochondria

The energy floor. Cells get the raw material to produce energy again before anything is asked of them.

A
Pillar two

Absorption and Detox

Stomach acid, bile flow, the gut barrier and the drainage pathways. Food breaks down and waste leaves.

R
Pillar three

Resilience

Nervous system and stress load. A body in fight or flight does not prioritise digestion, repair or fat loss.

C
Pillar four

Cycle and Signal

Hormones, thyroid and the signalling that was suppressed while inflammation was running the show.

H
Pillar five

Healthspan and Strength

Training, body composition and the habits that hold the result without a coach standing over them.
