What sets us apart

Straight answers.

Understand the approach.
Then decide if it feels right.

My doctor said my labs are normal. Why would this be different?

Standard reference ranges are built from population averages, and those populations include a lot of unwell people. Functional ranges ask a different question: where does a marker sit when the body is actually thriving, not just when it's short of a diagnosis.

A TSH of 4.2 is "normal." It can still explain the fatigue, the hair thinning, the cold hands. We read every marker against our in-house functional ranges, so we see the drift years before it becomes a diagnosis.

How is this different from an MD visit or a group coaching app?

A standard MD visit reads standard ranges in a 15-minute window and adjusts every six months, if that. A group coaching app hands you a static, generic plan with no one owning your case.

With us you get full functional bloodwork interpretation, one coach who owns your case, a protocol adjusted weekly or biweekly, and a team that stays in the case for 3 to 12 months and works alongside your physician. The result is a full rebuild, not symptom triage and not another template.

What actually makes the MARCH Method different?

Most programs start at hormones and thyroid, because that's what people ask for. We start at the floor. Mitochondria, then absorption, then resilience, and only then hormones. Sequence is the whole point. Hormone work stacked on a broken energy floor or a gut that can't absorb comes undone in weeks.

Can I actually get the lab testing I need?

Yes. We hold direct access to private lab testing across the US, Canada, and the UK. For Canadians, where private functional testing is genuinely hard to access, we open that door, and we have in-house nursing access to handle your draw inside Canada.

Who actually reads my bloodwork and builds my plan?

Farren reads every client's bloodwork personally and signs off on every protocol before it ships. Your designated coach walks with you day to day. Jimmy and Chan bring case-specific depth on autoimmune, gut, hormone, and skin patterns behind the scenes. One coach walking with you. Three brains on the file.

Are you doctors? Do you prescribe?

No. We're certified health coaches and never position ourselves as physicians. We don't diagnose, prescribe, or treat disease. We help you understand your data, build a protocol to move your markers toward optimal, and give you the language to have sharper conversations with your medical team. Any medication decision belongs to you and your physician.

What happens after I apply?

Farren reads every application personally. If it looks like a fit, you'll get a booking link within 48 hours for a real conversation with our Client Success Director. If it's not a fit, we'll tell you that too, and point you toward what will help.
